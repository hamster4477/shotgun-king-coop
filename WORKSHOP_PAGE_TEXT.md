## English

The Black King has a shotgun. His last loyal Knight has a blade. Play through Throne, Endless or Charnier together, with new cards that reward fighting side by side and hidden endings to discover.

[b]A hint for curious players[/b]
[spoiler]What happens when Revolution meets Guillotine? Who are the Bounty Cavalry hunting under that Warrant? And what exactly answers the call of Knight Phantom?[/spoiler]

[b]On one PC[/b]
Subscribe, enable this mod, and choose a local Co-op mode under Play. If you have the old standalone co-op mod installed, disable it first.

[b]On two Windows PCs · First-time setup[/b]
1. Both players subscribe to this item. Once Steam finishes downloading it, close the game.
2. In the Steam library where Shotgun King is installed, open [b]steamapps/workshop/content/1972440/3833493529[/b].
3. On each PC, run [b]Configure Steam Launch.vbs[/b], select the Steam account you play on, then click [b]Apply and restart Steam[/b]. After that, launch Shotgun King from Steam as usual.

[b]Host and join[/b]
Both PCs must be able to reach each other. Use the same LAN, join the same virtual LAN, or have the host set up a TCP tunnel. Both players open Play and select the same LAN mode. For Throne, choose [b]LAN Co-op[/b].

[b]Host (Black King):[/b] If several addresses appear, select the one your friend can reach. Click [b]Create room[/b], then [b]Copy invite[/b], and send the whole code to your friend.
[b]Guest (Black Knight):[/b] Copy the code, click [b]Paste[/b] beside the input field, then [b]Join[/b]. Once connected, the host chooses a weapon and rank and starts the run.

[b]Using a TCP tunnel?[/b] The host forwards the tunnel to the local address and port shown in the lobby, then enters the public address and port in the lobby’s optional tunnel settings before creating the room. The guest joins with the invite code as usual.

[b]Feedback[/b]
Report bugs or suggest changes on [url=https://github.com/hamster4477/shotgun-king-coop/issues]GitHub Issues[/url], or email hamster4477@qq.com. For bugs, include your role (King or Knight), the floor, and what happened just before the issue.

## 简体中文

黑王使用霰弹枪，最后一名仍追随他的黑骑士使用刀锋。两人可以一起挑战王座、无尽和剿灭模式，搭配围绕枪与刀与新玩家设计的新卡牌，也可以试着找出隐藏结局。

[b]给好奇的玩家一点线索[/b]
[spoiler]「革命」遇上「断头台」，会发生什么？「通缉令」上的人，真能逃过「悬赏骑兵」吗？至于「机关号令」……是谁在回应那道命令？[/spoiler]

[b]同一台电脑玩[/b]
订阅并启用本模组，在「游玩」里选择本地合作模式即可。

[b]两台 Windows 电脑联机 · 首次设置[/b]
1. 两个人都订阅本条目，等 Steam 下载完成后退出游戏。
2. 打开安装《霰弹国王》的 Steam 库，进入 [b]steamapps/workshop/content/1972440/3833493529[/b]。
3. 双方各自在自己的电脑上双击 [b]Configure Steam Launch.vbs[/b]，选中平时玩游戏的 Steam 账号，点「应用并重启 Steam」。以后直接从 Steam 启动游戏即可。

[b]创建和加入房间[/b]
两台电脑需要能互相访问。在同一局域网里可以直接连接；异地游玩时，请自行选择组网工具，先加入同一个虚拟局域网，或由房主配置 TCP 内网穿透。双方进入「游玩」，选择同一种局域网模式。想玩王座，就都选「局域网合作」。

[b]房主（黑王）[/b]　如果有多个本地地址，先选朋友能连到的那个。点「创建房间」→「复制连接码」，把整段发给朋友。
[b]加入者（黑骑士）[/b]　复制连接码，点输入框右侧的「粘贴」，再点「加入」。连上后，由房主选武器和难度并开始游戏。

[b]用樱花 FRP 等穿透工具？[/b] 房主先把 TCP 隧道转发到大厅显示的本地地址和端口，再到「穿透设置（可选）」填入对外地址和端口，返回大厅创建房间。朋友照常用连接码加入。

[b]问题反馈[/b]
遇到问题或有玩法建议，欢迎到 [url=https://github.com/hamster4477/shotgun-king-coop/issues]GitHub Issues[/url] 留言，也可以发邮件到 hamster4477@qq.com。反馈故障时，请写明你玩的是黑王还是黑骑士、所在楼层，以及出问题前做了什么。
